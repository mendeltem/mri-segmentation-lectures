Segmentation of the brain on MRI

A lecture series of short narrated films, one idea per step. English, 720p. Films 6 (U-Net and nnU-Net) and 8 (QC across all tools) follow.

01 MRI basics for segmentation

T1, T2, FLAIR and T2*, voxels and partial volume, bias field and N4, brain extraction, registration to MNI. 7:29

02 FreeSurfer recon-all

From the scan to aseg and the surfaces, the files and the log, and quality control: Euler number, slices, what to fix or exclude. 9:04

03 SynthSeg

Why contrast-specific models break, domain randomisation, robust mode, the quality scores, and where it fails. 7:53

04 Grey matter volumes in practice

A segmentation is a list, head size and three ways to adjust for it, lesions that pretend to be grey matter, QC. 8:39

05 White matter hyperintensities and BIANCA

A classifier, not a filter: data preparation, training labels, probability and threshold, validation and failure modes. 8:28

07 Detecting cerebral microbleeds

Blooming on T2*, look-alikes, three size classes (<2, 2-5, >5 mm), two-stage detection, MicrobleedNet, F1 vs Dice, QC. 8:42

Brain images: public VALDO 2021 challenge data (Sudre et al., "Where is VALDO? VAscular Lesions Detection and segmentatiOn challenge at MICCAI 2021", Medical Image Analysis 2024), used for teaching, non-commercial. Facts are taken from the method papers and official documentation named in each film. Source on GitHub
